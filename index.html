(About)

Born and raised in Palawan, Philippines — among lush forests, pristine beaches and crystal-clear water, she learned to see the world with wonder.

It began with a simple point-and-shoot camera and the beauty of her hometown. As she grew older, the curiosity turned to film — and to the road: from the rolling hills of Tuscany to the rugged landscapes of Kyrgyzstan.

Her photographs are a celebration of light, contrast and imperfection, revealing the soul of each place she visits — images that carry the viewer somewhere else entirely.
